The Game: DoTell

An open world that listens. NPCs who talk about you behind your back.

DoTell is our in-house game project: an open-world adventure built on a custom engine with a living social simulation. NPCs gossip — word of your deeds (and failures) travels from village to village, prices shift, doors open, and grudges stick. Quest performance and campaign choices reshape the world's factions, rumor economy and how strangers greet you.

  • Open world with a notable, hand-crafted tutorial
  • Gossip system — NPCs exchange rumors about the player
  • Adaptive world — reacts to behavior, quests & campaign activity
  • Built with Vaderporium assets — everything in the store is battle-tested in our own scenes
